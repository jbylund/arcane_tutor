TIER 1 -- a `provably_empty` signal per acquire branch, checked before dispatch.

SHAPE. Each acquire branch sets the flag from what IT can prove, never reconstructed downstream:
    plane                 the popcount IS the exact count and the residual is True
    candidates            an empty MATERIALIZED candidate list -- a structural argument, not a
                          counted one, and the only branch of the five that argues that way
    printing_compose      est.result.<space>.proven() == Some(0), the proven channel only
    the two bare ranges   k == 0 from the index partition points
`run_query_routed` answers (0, vec![]) on the flag without choosing or dispatching.

CORRECTNESS -- solid.
    4,000 routed queries compared against forced-plan totals:      0 mismatches
    8,000 queries, bench grading the SHIPPED flag:                 0 lies
    coverage identical to the matches-based numbers (952, 51.3%), so flag and count agree exactly
    310 debug / 307 release tests, clippy -D warnings clean, ruff clean

LATENCY -- no win. Paired A/B, 363 identical provably-empty queries, 9 reps each, median per query:
    before p50 20.88us      after p50 20.62us
    delta   p10 -2.17us     p50 +0.17us     p90 +2.04us
    total   8,685us -> 8,625us  (-0.7%)
The distribution straddles zero. That is noise, not a win.

WHY, and it should have been predicted: the ACQUIRE dominates at ~20us p50, and the execution being
skipped was already nearly free -- the executors short-circuit on an empty candidate list anyway, so
dispatch was 1-2us at most. "11.4% of queries answered with zero execution" was true about COVERAGE
and misleading about SAVINGS: there was nothing expensive to skip.

This also re-reads the number that motivated the item. The 9.3% of routing loss on empty-result
queries is a time-weighted HIT RATE across plans whose absolute times are all microseconds. It ranked
the defect; it never said the defect was worth much. The real time in item A is the other half -- the
74 queries that pay a full compose build before refusing, 3.59% of all measured time -- and those are
NOT provably empty, so this fastpath never covered them.

KEPT anyway, deliberately and labelled: sound, no measurable regression, and the flag is wanted by
the cost-model half. It does NOT earn its place on latency grounds and should not be cited as if it did.

WHY printing_compose PROVES SO FEW (43 of 733 empty compose queries, uniform sampler)

All 690 misses have and_mechanism = (none). Not one joint mechanism fired on any of them.
Their estimates are tiny: p10 1, p50 7, p75 34, p90 94, max 5,942.
    estimate <= 1      195 (28%)      <- post-stage-4 floor: the raw product rounded to 0
    estimate <= 5      321 (47%)
    estimate <= 20     464 (67%)
    estimate <= 100    624 (90%)

The reason is structural, not a gap to be patched. Compose is the ONLY route that never looks at the
intersection: `candidates` materializes a list and `plane` popcounts a bitmap, so both see emptiness
for free as a byproduct of work they were doing anyway. Compose estimates from MARGINALS -- that
cheap acquire is its whole reason for existing -- and an independence product of two non-empty
marginals is never zero. So compose can only prove zero via a mechanism that knows the JOINT:
leaves_are_disjoint (structural contradictions), the pair/subtype/arith tables (covered pairs), or
PlanePopcount. None covered any of the 690: `watermark:goblinexplosioneers border:borderless` is
neither contradictory nor tabled -- its emptiness is an empirical fact about the data.

Sharper version, visible in the estimate distribution: these are RARE x RARE conjunctions, which is
exactly the population the top-N pair tables exclude BY CONSTRUCTION -- rare pairs fall into
`rest_max` instead of getting their own entry. The pairs that most need an exact joint answer are the
ones the tables are designed not to store.

FIXABLE, but temper it: 67% have an estimate <= 20, so materializing the intersection at acquire time
would be bounded work. The payoff for the FASTPATH is likely the same null Tier 1 measured, since the
skipped work is cheap either way. The un-measured argument is the other one -- it would replace a
guess of 7 with an exact 0 in the cost model's input.
